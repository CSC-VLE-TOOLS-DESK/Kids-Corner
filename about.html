About Us · हमारे बारे में

Kids Corner is a free learning space for children in KG, UKG and Class 1. Children play 38 picture games in English and Hindi to learn letters, numbers, counting, simple words, opposites, days, shapes and colours.

किड्स कॉर्नर KG, UKG और कक्षा 1 के बच्चों के लिए मुफ़्त सीखने की जगह है, जहाँ तस्वीरों वाले खेलों से अक्षर, गिनती, शब्द, आकार और रंग सीखे जाते हैं।

Our goal

Make early learning fun, simple and free, so that every child can practise at home, at school or at a computer centre.

Part of SevaSphere

Kids Corner is a project of SevaSphere, which offers free tools for students, parents, teachers and service centres. Class 2 to 5 learning is on SevaSphere Kids Classes.
