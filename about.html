About Us · हमारे बारे में

Kids Corner is a free learning website for children from KG to Class 5. It has picture games, short lessons and quizzes in English and Hindi for letters, numbers, words, shapes, science and general knowledge.

किड्स कॉर्नर KG से कक्षा 5 के बच्चों के लिए मुफ़्त सीखने की वेबसाइट है, जिसमें खेल, पाठ और क्विज़ हैं।

Our goal

Make early learning fun, simple and free, at home, at school or at a computer centre.

Part of SevaSphere

Kids Corner is a project of SevaSphere – CSC VLE Tools Desk, which offers free tools for students, parents, teachers and service centres.
