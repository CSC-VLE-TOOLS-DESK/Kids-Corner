About Kids Corner

Kids Corner is a free learning website for children from KG to Class 5. It has 38 picture games for KG, UKG and Class 1 in English and Hindi, and short lessons with quizzes for KG to Class 5 with read-aloud and stars.

It is made by Hawa Singh Chauhan, CSC VLE Tools Desk, the team behind SevaSphere, a free set of online tools for CSC centres, shops and offices. Kids Corner now has its own website so that children and parents can come straight to the games.

There is no sign-up and no advertising. Stars and scores are saved only in your own browser on your own device.
